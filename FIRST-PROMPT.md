# Your first prompt

Copy everything in the box below and paste it into Claude Code with this repository selected. Fill in the parts in [brackets] first, or leave them and Claude will ask.

```
Read CLAUDE.md, then help me build the first version of my website.

Who I am: [one or two sentences: your name, what you do, where]
Who the site is for: [the people you most want to read it]
What I want them to do: [email me, read my writing, book a call, find my books…]
A site I like the look of: [a link]

Before you build, ask me up to five short questions, one at a time,
about anything you need (photos, links, the words I want to use).
Don't make up facts about me. Then build the home page (start from the link page in src/page.html and EDIT-ME.txt, or replace it if I want more),
push it to main, and send me the live link so I can look on my phone.
```

After that, just talk to it: "warmer colors", "add a section about my book", "use this photo" (drag the photo into the chat).
